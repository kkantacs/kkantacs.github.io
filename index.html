About Me

I am a PhD student (Senior Research Fellow) at the Cryptology and Security Research Unit (CSRU), Indian Statistical Institute, working under the guidance of Prof. Goutam Paul. My research focuses on the design and analysis of hybrid quantum-classical algorithms, including variational quantum algorithms, quantum kernel methods, and quantum machine learning for real-world problems.
